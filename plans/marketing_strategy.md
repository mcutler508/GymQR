# RepTag — Marketing Strategy

**South Jersey / Philadelphia / Philadelphia suburbs · weight-room gyms**

This document consolidates product positioning, ideal customer profile, geographic focus, pipeline stages, channel mix, and a practical execution horizon. It is intended for planning and execution; it does not replace shipping product or billing readiness.

---

## 1. Product summary (from repo positioning)

| Item | Direction |
|------|-----------|
| **Name** | RepTag |
| **Tagline / hook** | *Your gym remembers your lifts.* |
| **Frame** | **Member engagement and retention infrastructure** tied to each piece of equipment — **not** “another workout tracker.” |
| **Primary customer** | Gym owner / operator |
| **Primary user** | Member (scan → see last lift on **that** machine → log a set in seconds) |
| **Proof mechanic** | Physical QR on the machine; demo must work on **cellular** (real gym floor) |

**Monetization direction (planning):** SaaS **per gym location**; illustrative bands ~$99 / ~$299 / $500+ by size; **members free** in the initial model. Pricing levers: locations, equipment count, active members, branding, analytics depth.

**Privacy story (selling point):** Owners see **aggregate** analytics; **individual member lift history stays private** by default — reduces “surveillance” objection.

**Scope note:** Align all outbound and landing promises with **what is actually deployed** (full multi-tenant owner/member flows vs. narrower demo). Mismatch drives churn and refund risk.

## 2. Target geography

**Markets:** Fitness centers and gyms in **South Jersey**, **Philadelphia**, and **Philadelphia suburbs** — prioritizing venues with **weight lifting equipment** as opposed to yoga-only, Pilates-only, spin-only, or pure group-class studios.

**Paid targeting:** Use **radius or ZIP clusters** around, for example:

- **Philadelphia:** Center City, University City, Northeast Philly  
- **Suburbs:** King of Prussia / Main Line corridor, Conshohocken / Plymouth Meeting area  
- **South Jersey:** Cherry Hill, Mt. Laurel, Moorestown, Deptford, Glassboro (extend or trim by drive time)  
- **Optional spill:** Wilmington, DE, if Delaware-side leads are acceptable  

**Execution note:** Do not treat “Philadelphia” as a single blob. **City vs. suburbs** differ (parking, owner type, chain vs. independent, who answers the phone). Consider **separate creative or landing variants** where useful (“floor in Philly” vs. “suburban members”).

## 3. Ideal customer profile (ICP)

**Strong fit**

- **Boutique / strength-focused / hybrid** gyms where **equipment identity matters** (e.g. “Rack 4” vs. generic “squat”).  
- **Owner-operators** who can approve a **$99–299/mo** (or your actual price) line item without long procurement.  
- Facilities with a **real weight floor**: racks, benches, plate-loaded machines, multiple stations worth labeling.

**Prioritized gym types (weight-room focus)**

| Priority | Examples | Why |
|----------|----------|-----|
| High | Powerlifting / barbell clubs, hardcore strength gyms, athletic performance facilities | Equipment is the product; members think in sets on **this** rack. |
| High | Big-box or regional gyms with a **serious free-weight zone** | Volume of machines; pilot can start as **one zone**. |
| Medium | Hybrid “gym + classes” with a **dedicated weight floor** | Qualify on racks / plate-loaded lines, not class schedule. |
| Deprioritize (v1) | Yoga, Pilates, spin-only, bootcamp-only without a real weights area | Low sticker ROI unless they add a **dedicated strength corner**. |

**Qualification (one question):** *Roughly how many pieces of strength equipment would you put a sticker on — racks, benches, plate-loaded machines?* Vague answers or “we’re mostly classes” → deprioritize.

**Weaker early fit:** Prospects who need **full billing, scheduling, and CRM replacement** on day one — unless RepTag is explicitly **additive** to Mindbody, ABC, etc., not rip-and-replace.

## 4. Messaging pillars

- Lead with **outcome on the floor** — *last lift on this machine*, **fast log**, **under ~10 seconds** — not the word “QR” in the headline.  
- **Suburbs:** convenience, consistency, members who **won’t open an app** between sets.  
- **City:** density, differentiation — **your floor, your equipment IDs**.  
- **Cross-bridge corridor:** **Local proof** beats national claims — one **named** gym in Cherry Hill or King of Prussia matters more than generic scale.  
- **Objection — “We already have an app”:** This is for people who **won’t open an app between sets**; it’s **contextual at the machine**.

---

## 5. Pipeline stages (aligned to RepTag)

1. **Awareness** — Problem-led: retention, floor habit, differentiation vs. chains with apps. Asset-led: **short video** of scan → last lift → log.  
2. **Interest** — **Interactive demo**: printed QR, **cellular**, no Wi-Fi assumption.  
3. **Consideration** — **Pilot** on a small set of equipment (e.g. 3–10 machines), 2–4 weeks; success = scan counts + members using it **without** heavy training.  
4. **Decision** — Simple commercial: **per location**; optional caps on equipment or members until billing is fully self-serve.  
5. **Expansion** — More machines, second room, then second location.

**Pilot metric:** Pick **one** primary KPI (e.g. scans per week on pilot equipment, or share of active members who scan once) so renewal conversations stay simple.

## 6. Channels (general + this geography)

| Channel | Role for RepTag |
|---------|------------------|
| **Hyper-local outbound + in-person demo** | Highest close rate: curated list, **sticker in hand**, 15-minute scan demo. Zip-code the list **by week** (e.g. Montco week, South Jersey week). |
| **Instagram / Facebook (geo)** | Radius around **retail / industrial strips** where iron gyms cluster; creative = **phone POV** on a rack. Interest stacks: powerlifting, bodybuilding; CrossFit only if open gym + barbells. |
| **LinkedIn** | Owner titles: gym owner, studio owner, strength & conditioning — **demo clips** and problem/solution. |
| **Partners** | Equipment dealers, **used equipment** sellers, gym flooring installers, **local supplement** shops near hardcore gyms — they hear “opening / upgrading” early. |
| **Events** | Local meets, strongman, college barbell networks — founder brand and **referrals**, not always the direct customer. |
| **SEO / content (long game)** | One sharp pillar + “not replacing your member management system”; case studies unlock **comparison** and **local** search. |
| **Google Search** | Often **supplemental** early; noisy keywords burn budget. Stronger once **named pilots** and **proof pages** exist. |

**Cold paid ads:** Often underperform **without local proof**. Prefer **outbound + partnerships** first; use ads to **retarget** visitors and **amplify** a **local winner**.

## 7. List-building (practical)

- Map searches: **powerlifting gym**, **barbell club**, **strength and conditioning** + target towns.  
- **Drive the corridor:** Rough filter via **street view** — multiple racks visible.  
- **Chains:** Aim for **GM or single-location pilot** at a **high free-weight** site before corporate.

---

## 8. Gaps before scaling paid spend

- **Billing:** Self-serve signup + payment should match the ad promise once Stripe (or equivalent) is live.  
- **Landing / credibility:** Minimal public landing converts poorly on **cold** traffic; add **social proof, pilot terms, FAQ** as you invest in demand gen.  
- **Story vs. build:** Marketing must match **deployed** features.

---

## 9. 90-day execution focus (suggested)

**Objective:** ~10 qualified conversations, **3 pilots**, **1** reference customer you can **name** on a Philly/SNJ proof block.

| Phase | Actions |
|-------|---------|
| **Weeks 1–2** | Build **80–120** target accounts; tag city / suburb / South Jersey; **A-list ~40** with clear weight floors. |
| **Weeks 3–8** | Visits + DMs to A-list; push every serious conversation toward **“One QR on your busiest rack for a week.”** |
| **Ongoing** | One **30–45s case study clip** (cellular scan) from the first **yes**. |
